Our story

We built Nexora AI to end the busywork that eats good teams alive.

It started with a spreadsheet. Our founders were running operations at a mid-size logistics company, watching two analysts spend their afternoons copying order data between a shipping system and an accounting tool that refused to talk to each other. They wrote a script to move the data automatically. Then another, for approvals stuck in email threads. Within a year, the internal tool was doing more work than the team it was built to support.

That tool became Nexora AI. We build automation that sits on top of the software your team already runs, no rip and replace, no six month rollout. Approvals route themselves. Data moves between systems without a human copying it by hand. The people on your team get their hours back for the decisions that actually need a person behind them.
