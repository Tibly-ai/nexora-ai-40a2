What Nexora automates

The busywork disappears from your day.

We build directly on top of the tools you run today. Nothing gets ripped out, nothing gets replaced. It just starts moving on its own.

Approval routing

Purchase orders, expense reports and contracts get sent to the right person automatically, with reminders that follow up so nothing sits in an inbox.

Data entry and sync

Records move between your CRM, spreadsheets and finance software without anyone retyping a single field.

Customer onboarding

New accounts get provisioned, welcome sequences fire and internal teams get notified the moment a deal closes.

Invoice processing

Invoices get read, matched to purchase orders and queued for payment, flagged only when something looks off.

Support ticket triage

Tickets get tagged, prioritized and routed to the right queue before a human ever opens them.

Reporting and alerts

Daily and weekly reports assemble themselves, and anomalies trigger an alert to the right channel the moment they happen.
